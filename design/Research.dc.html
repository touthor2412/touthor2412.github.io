Teaching a computer to JUDGE KOI

Kohaku koi are white fish with red patterns, and in Japan a beautiful one can win prizes. Judging them takes years of experience. I'm testing whether an AI model can learn what a high-quality Kohaku looks like.

Why it matters

Koi are a big part of Niigata's culture and its local industry. A tool like this could one day help breeders and buyers by giving a second opinion that is consistent and easy to explain.

For the curious

The model is a Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Model (DDPM). The gap between the photo and the rebuilt fish, called the reconstruction error, is the quality signal.
